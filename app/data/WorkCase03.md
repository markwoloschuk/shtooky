THE SITUATION

[paragraph]
Flexport had chosen a unique location for its latest software release. SHiFT Midtown features multiple screens and projectors across its two floor environment. How could we use this space to best support the CEO’s presentation from the other side of the country?

[label]
THE SOLUTION

[paragraph]
Flexport makes software that supports global shipping and commerce. That’s global traffic in the sea, land and air. Those environments were the foundation for our visual creative. Our strategic goal was clear – to create a tightly integrated keynote presentation that would seamlessly move between slides, animations and video content. It needed flexible timing that would support the CEO’s inspirational tendency to veer off-script.

But we went beyond the presentation itself. We leveraged all the available video screens to create ambient media and digital signage. We added an evolution to the media that matched the emotional arc of the presentation. Ambient elements evolved from the pre-keynote anticipation, to supporting the presentation before settling into the post-keynote afterglow. During the presentation key script moment were reinforced with 360 degree animations that wrapped around the entire room.

To help the team and client iterate through production I created a 3D mockup of the space onto which we could rapidly map our videos and keynote. It wasn’t just a planning tool – it helped communicate our evolving vision to the client and allowed for faster reaction to feedback. Since they too are based on the West Coast everyone needed to be in alignment since they would only gain access to the space the day before.

THE SITUATION
[paragraph]
HP needed a video to support its booth at a busy sales conference. With so much audio-visual over-stimulation and every booth competing for attention how could we help them get their message across?

[pullquote]
Small bites of content let[br]
people <choose> what to consume.

[label]
THE SOLUTION
[paragraph]
The original request for a large screen video seemed doomed to failure. There was too much content they wanted to cover and too much competition for eyeballs. My solution was simple – not one big video – lots of smaller ones.

As so often is the case, less was more. We pitched the client on a booth of interactive kiosks. Conference attendees could pick and choose short videos on the topics they cared about and be rewarded with a choice of schwag after completing a short quiz. Win for them – win for the clients.

The content was mostly coming from HP subject matter experts. To keep it short and engaging we interviewed our subjects with an Errol Morris-style Interrotron camera setup. This creative use of teleprompting mirrors allows the subject and interviewer to lock eyes through the camera. The effect is a more emotional engagement that feels different from the typical talking head style interview.
